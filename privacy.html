Privacy Policy

How Dealkly collects, stores, and protects subscriber information.

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Information Collection

Dealkly collects only the information needed to deliver deal alerts.

  • Free subscribers: An email address entered into the Dealkly signup form.
  • Premium subscribers: An email address and optional watchlist keywords provided through Gumroad.
  • Automated monitoring data: Product titles, prices, links, and images from public eBay listings.

Dealkly does not collect passwords, payment card details, or banking information. Premium payments are processed entirely by Gumroad.

Data Storage and Security

Free subscriber email addresses are stored in a Dealkly subscriber list hosted in a GitHub repository [digital space].

Premium subscriber email addresses are stored in a separate Dealkly subscriber list [digital space]. Active premium subscriptions are synced from Gumroad using the Gumroad API.

Access to automated subscriber updates is protected by GitHub tokens and application secrets. Email delivery uses Gmail with application-level authentication.

Dealkly does not store or request payment credentials.

Tracking and Third-Party Sharing

Dealkly does not sell, rent, or trade subscriber email addresses.

Third-party services used to operate Dealkly include:

  • Google Forms / Google Apps Script — free signup processing.
  • Gumroad — premium subscription billing.
  • Gmail — email delivery.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.

Google’s handling of form data is governed by Google’s Privacy Policy.

eBay product links, prices, and images are public marketplace data and are not associated with a subscriber’s identity.

User Rights and Deletion

Every Dealkly email includes an unsubscribe link. Clicking that link immediately removes the subscriber from the free alert list.

Premium subscribers can cancel through Gumroad. After cancellation, the automated synchronization removes the subscriber from the premium alert list.

To request manual deletion or confirm that an email address has been removed, use the contact form.
